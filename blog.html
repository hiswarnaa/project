How I Fell in Love with Books

#Books #Reading #Bookstagram #Fiction

I wasn’t always a reader. In fact, for a long time, books felt like heavy objects teachers forced into my bag. But somewhere between growing up, getting lost, and finding myself again, I discovered a world that existed quietly between the pages.


My love for books began on a day I still remember vividly. I picked up a novel out of pure boredom, expecting nothing, and within minutes, I felt myself slipping into another universe. It was magical—like the world paused so I could breathe somewhere else. That story didn’t just entertain me; it understood me. It showed me that books were not just paper and ink—they were emotions, places, and people I could carry with me


Slowly, reading became my escape. Whenever life got loud, books softened the noise. Whenever I felt misunderstood, a character somewhere mirrored my thoughts. Stories made me feel seen in ways real life sometimes couldn’t.


Over time, I began craving that peace—the calmness of turning pages, the excitement of cliffhangers, the smell of old paper, the comfort of fictional friendships. Books became a home I could return to anytime


Today, reading isn’t just a hobby; it’s a part of who I am. Books have taught me empathy, patience, imagination, and the beauty of slowing down. They’ve held my hand through heartbreaks, celebrated my victories, and shaped the way I view the world
