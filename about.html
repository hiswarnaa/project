About Readers

Hello, Reader!

Welcome to my cozy little corner of the internet. I created this book blog because stories have always felt like home to me. I love sharing recommendations, reviews, and small thoughts that books leave behind.

Whether it’s romance, fantasy, thrillers, or quiet literary fiction— there’s a book for every mood and a reader behind every story.

I started reading when I was young, and since then, books have shaped my personality, my dreams, and even the way I observe people. I believe reading doesn’t just teach you—it transforms you slowly, page by page.

This blog is simply a reflection of that love. If you're a fellow booklover, you’re already family here.

Types of Readers

The Emotional Reader

Cries easily, laughs loudly, gets attached to characters, and remembers lines forever.

The Aesthetic Reader

Collects pretty books, loves annotations, tabs, and takes cute book photos.

The Deep Reader

Reads slowly, underlines quotes, finds hidden meanings, and loves philosophical writing.

The Fast Reader

Finishes a 400-page book in one sitting and posts reviews immediately.

The Night Owl Reader

Reads only at night, loses sleep, survives on tea/coffee, and says “one last chapter” 13 times.
